# meterless Cookie Notice

Version 1.3. Effective 6 October 2026.

This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies used on meterless.com and app.meterless.com, what they do and how long they last. All of them are strictly necessary to provide what you ask for, and the law does not require consent for them. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, session replay or pixels.

## Strictly necessary

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| meterless_session | meterless, on app.meterless.com, when you sign in | Keeps you signed in to your account and studio | 7 days from sign-in |
| Security cookies such as __cf_bm, cf_clearance and _cfuvid | Cloudflare, our network provider, where its protections are active | Tell people apart from automated traffic and protect the app against attacks | From 30 minutes up to the period Cloudflare sets |
| g_state, and short-lived sign-in state | Google, where the Sign in with Google button is shown or used | Remembers your sign-in choice and protects the sign-in step | As Google sets |

**Not a cookie.** When you dismiss this notice, the app records that in your browser's session storage so the notice is not shown again in that tab. That record is never sent to us and is cleared when you close the tab.

**Your studio.** The tools in your studio, such as ComfyUI or Jupyter, run in your browser. They keep their own settings and work in progress in your browser's storage, and some set their own cookies. They are part of the tool you open and are needed for it to work.

## The marketing site

meterless.com sets no cookies of its own.

## Paddle

When you pay, Paddle's checkout opens in a frame served by Paddle, our reseller. Paddle sets its own cookies within the checkout and may collect information about your device to prevent fraud, under [Paddle's own privacy and cookie notices](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy). We do not control them.

## Managing cookies

Your browser lets you view, block and delete cookies. Blocking them will sign you out, or stop the studio tools or the checkout from working.

## Changes

We update this notice when the cookies we or our providers set change. If we ever want to use a cookie that needs your consent, we will ask for it before setting it.
