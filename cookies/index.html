This notice lists every cookie and similar item meterless places on your device, what it does, and how long it lasts. There is one category. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use session replay, pixels or fingerprinting.

Stripe

When you pay, you are on Stripe's checkout and billing pages. Stripe sets its own cookies on those pages for fraud prevention and to run the checkout, under Stripe's cookie policy at stripe.com/cookies-policy. We do not control them and they are not set on meterless pages.

Managing cookies

Your browser lets you view, block and delete cookies. Blocking the session cookie will sign you out. There is nothing else to manage, because there is nothing else set.

Changes

If we ever add a cookie, this notice is updated first, and if the new cookie would need your consent we will ask for it before setting it.
